Notes:
Thank you for reviewing Get Wattly.

Get Wattly connects to smart home systems on the user's home network (Philips Hue, Plugwise, Fibaro) or via the Samsung SmartThings cloud. A review team will normally not have this hardware, so the app contains a built-in DEMO HOME with simulated devices.

HOW TO TEST WITHOUT HARDWARE
1. Open the app. On the first screen tap "Look at the demo home first".
2. The app scans the demo home and shows the devices sorted by room. Tap "Assign 3 devices".
3. For each device, tap a room (in real use the light blinks so the user knows which one it is).
4. Tap "Open my home". You can now switch rooms and lights on/off, dim lights and change the thermostat.
5. Settings (gear icon, top right) shows the room layout, family mode and language.

IN-APP PURCHASE
- "7-day Trial" (non-consumable, free): starts the 7-day free trial.
- "Get Wattly – full version" (non-consumable, one-time): unlocks the app permanently after the trial.
- "Restore purchase" is available on the start screen and in Settings.
Nothing is charged automatically; there is no subscription.

LOCAL NETWORK
The app requests Local Network permission to find and control the user's own smart home devices. No data is collected by the developer.
